I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Economics at the University of Texas at Austin.

I am on the 2026-2027 job market.

My research interests lie in information economics, game theory, mechanism design, and contract theory. Broadly, I study how information shapes incentives, strategic interactions, and market outcomes. My current research focuses on the design of information and institutions under asymmetric information, with applications to contracts, organizations, and digital markets.

Working Papers

Job Market Paper

What to Measure: Optimal Monitoring under Adverse Selection and Moral Hazard

PDF Slides

Studies optimal monitoring when adverse selection and moral hazard coexist. The principal allocates monitoring resources between learning the agent's private type and observing the agent's hidden action, with implications for the design and value of information.

Work in Progress

Screening by Task Assignment

Studies contract design when agents can conceal or manipulate observed outcomes. Different tasks give agents different opportunities for manipulation, so task assignment changes the set of allocations that can be sustained through screening.

Work in Progress

Competition and Privacy in Digital Markets

Studies how competition shapes firms' incentives to collect, use, and disclose consumer information in digital markets.
